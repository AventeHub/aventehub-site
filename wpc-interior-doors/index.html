IN EVERY SET

The whole door set, factory finished

Complete set

Each set has the door leaf, the frame, the trim and the hardware: handle, hinges and lock set.

Standard size

Our quoted size is a 36 × 84 in (914 × 2134 mm) opening. Other sizes on request.

Factory finished

The face is a laminated wood grain or solid colour, so there is no painting on site.

THE DOOR PACK

Pairs with our wood-grain baseboards

Each door finish has a wood-grain aluminum baseboard to go with it.

  • Walnutwith the walnut door
  • Grey Oakwith the grey oak door
  • American Oakwith the light oak door
  • Silver Pearwith the taupe door
  • Ashwith the taupe door
  • Dark Walnutwith the charcoal door

Colours are matched by eye. See the door and the baseboard together on our sample board.
