THE FILM

LED Baseboards in one minute

HOW IT WORKS

Clip on. Never paint.

24 V strip inside

The baseboard is the channel. One LED strip runs the length of the wall; the driver sits in a cabinet or the wall box.

Clip on, after the floor

The clip base is screwed to the wall first. The profile snaps on at the end, over the finished floor.

One family of parts

Inside and outside corners, end caps and connectors in the same finish. No mitre cuts on site.

APPLICATIONS

Where developers use it

BedroomA low wash of light for the night: the room reads without a ceiling light.
Corridor cornerThe light turns the corner with the corner piece.
HallwayA line along the floor guides the way; no wall sconces to wire.
OfficeA clean side line under the wall panels.
Feature wallLight from the floor up a stone or panel wall.
Light onThe profile disappears, the line stays.
THE GRF STANDARD

Why it is GRF

Green

LED light at the floor from a 24 V strip: a few watts per metre in place of wall lights, and an aluminum profile that is recycled at the end.

Resilient

Extruded aluminum, alloy 6063-T5, with a factory finish. No swelling, no chipping; the light channel is part of the profile.

Fast

Clip base on the wall, strip in the channel, profile snaps on after the floor. Corners and caps are parts.
