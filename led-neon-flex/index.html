LED Neon Flex

A continuous line of light that bends around curves and corners, cuts to length at the marks, and shrugs off water.

We supply and deliver LED neon flex to developers and builders in the Greater Toronto Area and Florida, with sample boards brought to your site.

THE GRF STANDARD

Why it is GRF

Green

24 V LED light. The strip inside is rated up to 118 lm/W (supplier's figure).

Resilient

Co-extruded silicone with an anti-break core, rated IP65 to IP68 by model (supplier's ratings). Integral moulded plug on the outdoor model.

Fast

Cut at the marks, clip into the aluminium channel or the flexible profile, one feed per run. No wet work.

THE LIGHTING PACK

Pairs with our LED crown and aluminium channels

Each neon section has its own aluminium channel and clips. Inside, the neon takes the cove line of our LED crown moulding.

  • LED Crown Mouldingthe neon takes the cove line
  • Aluminium channel 14.5 × 10 mmfor AVH-NF01
  • Aluminium channel 22.5 × 14.5 mmfor AVH-NF02
  • Aluminium channel 19 × 18 mmfor AVH-NF03
  • Aluminium channel 12 × 9.5 mmfor AVH-NF04
  • Flexible profilefor curves, with the clips

Channels and clips are on the sample board with the neon.
