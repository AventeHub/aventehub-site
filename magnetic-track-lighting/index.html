Magnetic Track Lighting

A thin 24 V rail in the ceiling. Spots, linears and pendants snap on by magnet and move whenever the room changes. Two rails: recessed trimless, and ultra-slim surface.

We supply and deliver magnetic track lighting to developers and builders in the Greater Toronto Area and Florida, with sample boards brought to your site.

HOW IT WORKS

One rail. Any light. Move it.

24 V on the rail

Low voltage on the rail; one driver per run, hidden above the ceiling.

Held by magnet

Lift, move, add. No tools, and no electrician for a change after move-in.

Two rails, one family

Recessed trimless where the ceiling is new; ultra-slim surface where it is already finished. The same fixtures on both.

PLAN YOUR LAYOUT

Six shapes cover most rooms

Each starts as one rail and one driver; corners and crossings are plug-in parts. One driver per run: a 100 W driver carries fixtures up to 100 W in total, a 200 W driver up to 200 W.

Parallel linesTwo or three rails side by side across a living room or office ceiling. Even light, spots where the art is.
TOne rail along the corridor, one branch into the room. The three-direction module makes the join.
Full loopA rectangle around the ceiling edge. Light from four sides, pendants on the long run over the table.
LAlong two walls of a kitchen: linears over the counter, spots at the island corner.
Double LTwo L shapes facing each other, a gap between. Reads as a frame around the room without closing it.
UThree sides of the ceiling, open at the window wall. Good for a model suite where the view stays clear.

THE GRF STANDARD

Why it is GRF

Green

24 V DC on the rail. LED fixtures at 60 lm/W and CRI 90 (maker’s figures). The driver dims 10–100 %, so the light is only what the room needs.

Resilient

An aluminum rail that outlives the fit-out. Fixtures rated 50,000 h (maker’s figure). Swap fixtures, keep the rail.

Fast

One cut-in for the rail, one driver per run, fixtures snap on in seconds. Layout changes after move-in take no trades.
