THE GRF STANDARD

Why it is GRF

Green

24 V DC on the strip. Dimmable in three steps from the panel: 100, 50 and 20 %.

Resilient

Pixel-IC strip on a 10 mm board. Cuttable: the rest keeps working (supplier's data).

Fast

Double-sided 3M tape, plug-in connectors, RF wall panel in the standard 86 mm box. No programming.

THE LIGHTING PACK

Pairs with stairs and corridors

The running light does two jobs in a multi-unit building: it guides and it impresses.

  • Stair stringersthe light runs up the stair as you climb
  • Corridor ceilingsone run toward the exit
  • LED baseboard channelfor a floor-level run
  • Parking entry and rampa direction line
  • Elevator lobby ceilinga line that moves
  • Lobby feature wallslots in the wall panels

For a floor-level run, the strip sits in our LED baseboard channel.
