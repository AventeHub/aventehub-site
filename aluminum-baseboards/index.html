HOW IT WORKS

Clip on. Never paint.

Factory finish

Powder coat, brushed metal or wood grain, done at the factory. Nothing to paint on site, nothing to touch up later.

Clip on, after the floor

The clip base is screwed to the wall first. The profile snaps on at the end, over the finished floor, and hides the wire.

Corners are parts

Inside and outside corners, end caps and connectors in the same finish. No mitre saw on site.

THE GRF STANDARD

Why it is GRF

Green

Aluminum instead of MDF and paint: nothing to prime or spray on site, and the metal is recycled at the end of its life.

Resilient

Extruded aluminum, alloy 6063-T5, factory finish. Scratch resistant, waterproof, wipes clean. The line stays straight.

Fast

Clip base on the wall before the floor, profile on after it. Corners and caps are parts, not cuts.
